Why this exists

Most people do not have a portfolio problem. They have an organisation problem.

A family can have bank deposits, mutual funds, insurance, property, loans, vehicle papers and dozens of documents. The information usually ends up spread across emails, WhatsApp, provider apps, spreadsheets and paper files.

01

A due date is missed

The premium was in a message somewhere. The FD maturity date was written in a PDF. The EMI date was remembered only after the debit failed.

02

The document cannot be found

You know the policy exists, but the PDF, receipt or claim number is buried inside an old email or folder.

03

Only one person knows everything

The family may not know which policies, loans, nominees or investments exist when that information is actually needed.

Where your data lives

Cloud storage should be a choice, not a requirement.

The basic app is planned to keep your financial records on your phone. Cloud services are for people who want backup, multiple devices or family sharing.

Option 1

Phone only

Keep the main copy of your records encrypted on your phone.

  • No cloud account needed
  • PIN and biometric lock
  • Local reminders
Option 2

Your own backup

Keep an encrypted backup file in your own Google Drive or iCloud.

  • Backup without sharing
  • Encrypted before upload
  • Restore on a new phone
Option 3

Totalfolio cloud

Turn on cloud sync if you want family sharing, multiple devices or a web companion later.

  • Optional paid service
  • Encrypted records
  • Family permissions

What we will not do

A finance app should earn trust by asking for less.

01
No UPI PINs or banking passwords

Totalfolio does not need your login credentials to help you organise your records.

02
No ads

A personal finance organiser should not depend on advertising inside a user's financial records.

03
No investment advice

Totalfolio is intended to organise your information, not tell you which fund or share to buy.

04
No forced cloud account

Basic phone-only use should remain useful without signing up for cloud storage.
