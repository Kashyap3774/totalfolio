The problem

Personal finance is fragmented by default.

An individual's financial life may span banks, insurers, brokers, documents, emails, spreadsheets and physical records. The information exists, but there is rarely one neutral place to organize it.

01

Information is scattered

Investments, insurance, loans and important documents often live across unrelated providers and formats.

02

Important dates are easy to miss

Renewals, maturities, premiums and obligations are frequently tracked manually or remembered too late.

03

Families lack a shared financial map

Important financial information may be known by one person but not easily accessible to trusted family members when it matters.

Product

A structured home for the financial records that matter.

Totalfolio is being designed as an organizational layer over the financial products people already use — not as another bank, broker or insurer.

Privacy architecture

Storage should be a user's decision.

Totalfolio is being designed around the principle that sensitive personal financial information should not require cloud storage merely to make the product useful.

Local-first mode

Device

Financial records can remain on the user's device for people who prefer a local-only approach.

Optional cloud synchronization

Opt-in

Cloud services can be used when users need backup, synchronization or trusted family access.

Controlled document processing

Explicit

AI-assisted document processing is intended to be a deliberate user action rather than an invisible default.

Workflow

Simple enough to maintain over time.

A useful financial organizer should reduce administrative effort rather than create another system that needs constant maintenance.
