Why Totalfolio

Your financial life is bigger than your investment portfolio.

A family's finances can include investments, loans, insurance policies, bank deposits, property records, nominees and dozens of important dates. Most people manage them across multiple apps, PDFs, emails and paper folders.

01

Important dates get missed

Insurance renewals, FD maturities, loan payments and yearly financial tasks are easy to forget when they are tracked in different places.

02

Documents are scattered

Policies, statements and receipts often live across email, WhatsApp, physical folders and different provider apps.

03

Families do not know what exists

One person may understand the household finances while everyone else has little idea which policies, assets or liabilities exist.

Privacy is part of the product

You decide where your financial data lives.

The app is being designed so that basic use does not require uploading your financial records to Totalfolio's servers.

Mode 01

Phone only

Keep the master copy of your financial records encrypted on your own phone.

  • No cloud account required
  • Local reminders
  • PIN and biometric lock
Mode 02

Your own backup

Keep an encrypted backup file in your own Google Drive or iCloud account.

  • Backup without family sharing
  • Encrypted before upload
  • Restore to a new phone
Mode 03

Totalfolio cloud

Use encrypted cloud sync when you need multiple devices, web access or family sharing.

  • Optional paid feature
  • Encrypted records
  • Family permissions

Questions

A few things we want to make clear from the beginning.

No. Totalfolio is being built as a record-keeping, organisation and reminder tool. It is not intended to tell users what to buy or sell.
