Privacy by design

Your financial information should remain under your control.

Totalfolio is being designed around a local-first approach, giving users control over how and where their financial information is stored.

✓ Local-first storage option for users who prefer to keep information on their own device.
✓ Optional cloud synchronization for backup and multi-device access.
✓ User-controlled AI processing rather than silently sending financial information to external services.
✓ Granular sharing designed for individuals and trusted family members.

AI assistance

Making financial documents easier to organize.

We are exploring Claude-powered workflows to help users extract, structure and understand information from their own financial documents while keeping AI usage optional and transparent.

Example workflow
Upload an insurance policy or investment document → identify important fields such as policy details, maturity or renewal dates → review the extracted information → save it to the appropriate Totalfolio record → optionally create reminders.
