Why this is useful

Most families already have the information. The real problem is that it is scattered.

Some things are in Excel, some are in email, some are in WhatsApp, and some are only in one person's head. Totalfolio is meant to bring these records into one place that is easier to review on a phone.

01

Due dates get missed

Insurance renewals, electricity bills, IGL bills, EMIs and policy premiums need attention on time.

02

Records are hard to find

Policy numbers, payment receipts and document dates are often buried inside old files and messages.

03

Only one person knows everything

A spouse or parent may need access to key information but not know where the records are kept.

How this becomes a mobile interface Practical

Instead of a spreadsheet, each row becomes a usable record.

The app should not copy Excel blindly. It should convert that data into screens that are easier to update, review and remind from.

1
Each item gets its own record page

For example: one LIC policy page, one electricity bill page, one card page.

2
Important fields are shown first

Policy no, due next, due amount, last payment, maturity amount, statement date.

3
Status becomes visual

Paid, due soon, upcoming and overdue should be visible instantly.

4
Documents and receipts stay attached

This is much better than storing policy PDFs and screenshots separately.

5
Family use stays simple

A spouse or parent should be able to understand the record without opening a sheet.

Suggested record fields in the app MVP

Good first version fields

For launch, keep the record structure simple and close to what users already understand from their own manual sheets.

1
Common fields

Type, title, company, account or policy number, note, document.

Privacy

Financial records should stay under the user's control.

The free mode should still be useful even if a user keeps the data on the phone only. Cloud should be optional for backup, multiple devices and family sharing.

Option 1

Phone only

Keep records encrypted on the phone with PIN and biometric protection.

  • No cloud account required
  • Local reminders
  • Good for private use
Option 2

Your own backup

Store an encrypted backup in your own Google Drive or iCloud.

  • Encrypted before upload
  • Useful when changing phones
  • No shared vault needed
Option 3

Totalfolio cloud

Use sync only if you want multi-device access or family sharing.

  • Optional paid feature
  • Shared family access
  • Future web companion
