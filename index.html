Totalfolio

Your financial life, organized in one place.

Totalfolio is a privacy-first personal financial management platform for individuals and families.

It helps users organize investments, insurance policies, loans, fixed deposits, mutual funds, stocks, assets and important financial documents, while setting reminders for renewals, premiums, maturities and other important dates.

What we're building

  • Unified financial portfolio tracking
  • Insurance, loan and investment management
  • Smart reminders and alerts
  • Optional family sharing
  • Privacy-first local or cloud storage
  • AI-assisted financial document organization
